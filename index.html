Privacy and intended use

This prototype saves entries locally on this device, without an account or cloud synchronization. Anyone with access to the same Windows account or browser profile may be able to access the entries; uninstalling the Windows app is configured to erase its locally stored data. Export a copy first if needed. Do not enter sensitive patient information or rely on this app for medication reminders, clinical decisions, or emergency care. Always verify your medication instructions with your healthcare professional.
